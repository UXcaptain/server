## Campaign Structure Recommendations

### Campaign 1: Brand
- **Keywords**: Exact match brand terms
- **Budget**: 10-15% of total
- **Goal**: Protect brand, capture navigational intent

### Campaign 2: Competitor Conquesting
- **Keywords**: Competitor name + alternativa/comparativa
- **Budget**: 20-25% of total
- **Goal**: Capture users evaluating alternatives

### Campaign 3: Solution-Aware
- **Keywords**: Test usabilidad, research UX, herramienta análisis
- **Budget**: 35-40% of total
- **Goal**: Primary conversion driver

### Campaign 4: Problem-Aware
- **Keywords**: Budget pain points, GDPR concerns, time-saving
- **Budget**: 20-25% of total
- **Goal**: Educate and convert early-stage researchers

### Campaign 5: Display Remarketing
- **Audience**: Website visitors, video viewers
- **Goal**: Re-engage and convert
