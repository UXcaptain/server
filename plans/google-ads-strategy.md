# Google Ads Strategy for UXcaptain
## Video-Based User Research Platform - Spain Market

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** March 2025  
**Market Focus:** Spain (Primary), EU Secondary  
**Budget Tier:** Small-Medium Business (€500-€3,000/month initial)

---

## Executive Summary

UXcaptain is entering a competitive but opportunity-rich market. With 90% of Spanish UX professionals already collecting qualitative feedback but struggling with recruitment (25+ pain points), time investment (15+), and analysis (12+), there's clear demand for an AI-powered, cost-effective solution.

**Strategic Positioning:** Affordable AI-powered alternative to expensive enterprise tools (Maze, UserTesting) with focus on automated transcription and insight extraction.

**Key Differentiators:**
- AI-generated insight summaries (highest demand: 30+ requests)
- Price point aligned with Spanish market (35% spend <€1,000/year)
- Automated participant recruitment (post-MVP)
- 75% preference for external users = key value proposition
