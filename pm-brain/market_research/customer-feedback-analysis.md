# Customer Feedback Analysis & Pricing Recommendations

## 1. Executive Summary

This document synthesizes comprehensive market research conducted with 64 UX professionals in December 2025, combined with historical data from the "Estado del UX en España 2021" survey. The analysis provides strong 4-year validation for UXcaptain's core value proposition and informs the recommended pricing strategy.

### Key Findings

- **High Market Demand**: 90% of UX professionals collect qualitative feedback, with 75% preferring external, unbiased participants
- **Persistent Pain Points**: User recruitment remains the #1 challenge (25+ mentions across both studies), followed by time investment and tool costs
- **Feature Priorities Validated**: AI-generated insight summaries (30+ requests), recruitment delegation (20+ requests), and metric summaries (15+ requests) align perfectly with planned features
- **Budget Reality**: 35% of Spanish UX professionals spend less than €1,000/year on UX tools, requiring competitive pricing

### 4-Year Market Validation Summary

The convergence of data from 2021 and 2025 demonstrates consistent market needs:

| Pain Point | 2021 Mentions | 2025 Mentions | Trend |
|------------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| User Recruitment | 25+ | 25+ | Stable — #1 priority |
| Time Investment | 50+ | 15+ | Decreasing (improving awareness) |
| Tool Costs | 40+ | 10+ | Decreasing (market maturation) |
| Data Analysis | 30+ | 12+ | Decreasing (emerging solutions) |

## 3. Key Findings

### Pain Points (4-Year Validation)

The research consistently identifies the same critical pain points across both studies, validating UXcaptain's core problem statement:

#### 1. User Recruitment — #1 Pain Point (25+ mentions in both studies)
- "Encontrar usuarios para realizar tests"
- "Me cuesta encontrar usuarios dispuestos dar feedback"
- "Me cuesta encontrar usuarios (de fuera del equipo) dispuestos dar feedback"
- Most expensive and complex process in user research
- Critical bottleneck for research teams

#### 2. Time Investment — 50+ mentions (2021) → 15+ (2025)
- "Las deadlines no me permiten recoger feedback correctamente"
- "Poco tiempo para hacer buenos research"
- Analysis and synthesis takes too long
- Fast development cycles don't allow time for proper research
- Time pressure prevents thorough user validation

#### 3. Tool Costs — 40+ mentions (2021) → 10+ (2025)
- "El presupuesto dedicado por la empresa a herramientas de UX es muy bajo / inexistente"
- "El cliente final no está dispuesto a invertir en UX"
- Current tools too expensive for many organizations
- Difficulty finding effective qualitative feedback tools

#### 4. Data Analysis — 30+ mentions (2021) → 12+ (2025)
- "El feedback que recibo no es de calidad"
- "Es muy difícil encontrar feedback de calidad"
- Difficulty going beyond surface-level observations
- Risk of confirmation bias in analysis
- Manual synthesis is labor-intensive

### Feature Requests

The following features were requested by survey respondents, ranked by frequency:

#### 1. AI-Generated Insight Summaries (30+ requests)
- Summary of main user thoughts powered by AI
- Automated synthesis of key findings
- **Direct validation for UXCaptain's core value proposition**

#### 2. Participant Recruitment Delegation (20+ requests)
- Platform to handle user recruitment automatically
- Access to external, unbiased participants
- **Aligns with UXCaptain's planned participant system**

#### 3. Metric Summaries (15+ requests)
- Task success rate, time-on-task metrics
- Quantitative analysis of qualitative data
- **Supports UXCaptain's analytics dashboard feature**

#### 4. Question Generation (12+ requests)
- AI-powered question suggestions based on product
- Help defining proper research questions
- **Validates UXCaptain's task generation feature**

#### 5. Interview Capabilities (10+ requests)
- Ability to conduct moderated interviews
- Follow-up conversations with users after analysis
- **Supports planned moderated sessions feature**

### Market Reality

Understanding the target market is critical for positioning and pricing:

#### Budget Constraints
- **35%** spend less than €1,000/year on UX tools
- **30%** don't know or won't disclose tool spending
- **Only 8%** invest heavily (>€10,000/year)

#### Team Structure
- **45%** work in small teams (2-5 people)
- **35%** work in larger teams (>5 people)
- **20%** work as freelancers

#### Work Patterns
- **40%** work fully remote
- **35%** work hybrid
- **25%** work in-office

#### Research Habits
- **32%** "never" conduct research (significant opportunity)
- **45%** conduct "occasional" research
- **23%** always conduct research before design

#### Key Differentiator: External Users
- **75%** prefer external users who don't know their organization
- Reasons: avoid bias, get honest feedback, realistic perspective, reduce internal politics

## 5. Pricing Model

### Recommended Tier Structure

| Feature | Free (€0) | Starter (€49/mo) | Pro (€99/mo) |
|---------|-----------|------------------|--------------|
| **Own Users (sessions/month)** | 5 | 20 | 50 |
| **External Users (sessions/month)** | 1 | 5 | 15 |
| **AI Transcription** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **AI Insight Summaries** | — | Beta | Full |
| **Team Seats** | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| **Integrations** | — | — | ✓ |
| **Annual Cost** | €0 | €588 | €1,188 |

### Pricing Rationale

#### Competitor Benchmarks

| Competitor | Free | Entry Paid | Mid-Tier | Enterprise |
|------------|------|------------|----------|------------|
| Userbrain | Free | $99/mo | $199/mo | Custom |
| UXtweak | Free | €92/mo | €199/mo | Custom |
| Lookback | — | $25/mo | $149/mo | Custom |
| UserTesting | — | $49/mo | $399/mo | Custom |

#### Spanish Market Alignment

- **€49 Starter** undercuts UXtweak by 47% (€92 → €49)
- **€99 Pro** matches Userbrain Pro in EUR ($99 ≈ €99)
- **Both tiers under €1,000/year threshold** — where 35% of market sits
- **Transparent pricing** — vs. hidden enterprise pricing of competitors

### Free Tier Strategy

#### Why Include External Users in Free Tier?

1. **75% prefer external users** — this is the key differentiator
2. **Competitors include pool in Free** — Userbrain, UXtweak
3. **Enables conversion** — users experience the value before paying
4. **Low cost, high conversion value** — 1 session/month is minimal investment

#### Free Tier Objectives

- **Target 32% who "never" conduct research** — lower barrier to entry
- **Compete with Userbrain/UXtweak free offerings** — feature parity
- **Create habit before paid conversion** — freemium model success pattern

## 6. Own Users vs External Users

### Current Recommendation: Unified Model (MVP)

**MVP Phase: "Sessions/month" (Unified)**
- Simpler messaging and user understanding
- Clear consumption tracking
- Avoids confusion about user types

**Session Allowance by Tier:**

| Tier | Total Sessions/Month | Rationale |
|------|---------------------|-----------|
| Free | 5 own + 1 external = 6 | Showcase both capabilities |
| Starter | 20 | 4x Free, reasonable growth |
| Pro | 50 | 2.5x Starter, team scale |

### Post-MVP: Separate Model

After MVP, consider splitting into:

- **Own Users**: Unlimited (organization's own participants)
- **External Pool**: 5-15/month by tier (from participant pool)

### Why Include External in Free?

| Reason | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| 75% preference | Key differentiator — must showcase |
| Competitor parity | Userbrain includes external in Free |
| Conversion value | Users experience unique value proposition |
| Low marginal cost | 1 session is minimal investment |
